# NEO + SmartSearch — Complementary Capability Evidence

## Agreed product position

**SmartSearch does not replace NEO. It extends VNA's direct-channel capability.**
NEO continues to assist customers within the Vietnam Airlines website, while
SmartSearch demonstrates two complementary capabilities: handling a complex
multi-origin discovery request in one flow and exposing a future MCP channel
that authorised external AI assistants could call.

The comparison should therefore be framed as an observed journey gap and an
extension opportunity—not as an attack on NEO or a claim that SmartSearch is
universally “better”.

## Test prompt

> Book me a one-way trip to Hanoi near the end of the year, with a budget of
> around AUD 1,000 per person. Two adults depart from Sydney and two depart
> from Melbourne.

## Structural extension

In the tested journey, NEO is the customer assistant within the VNA website.
SmartSearch adds an MCP-ready search layer so the same VNA-authorised discovery
capability could later be reached from external AI assistants, while final
booking and payment remain with Vietnam Airlines.

**Pitch line:** *NEO supports travellers on the VNA website; SmartSearch does
not replace it—it extends VNA's intelligent direct channel to complex journeys
and, with VNA authorisation, to external AI assistants.*
